      03 — Built from the environment

      I'm Du
      Antonio

      I'm a Toronto-born artist and entrepreneur, and Screw Face Product is a brand built from real experiences, culture, and the environments that shaped me.

      Growing up between Toronto, East Atlanta and Broward County gave me three different environments and everything that came with them. SFP started as a personal statement about that — a reflection of where I come from, what I've been through, and who I've become.

      What started as my identity became something bigger: a brand for anyone shaped by where they come from who refuses to be limited by it.

      Du Antonio — Founder, SFP
