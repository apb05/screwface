      Screw Face Product is a lifestyle brand born out of Toronto and shaped by the environments, experiences and culture that influence who we become.

      01 — Who we are

      What started as a personal statement has grown into a brand for people who move differently.

      Founded by artist and entrepreneur Du Antonio, SFP represents individuality, authenticity, and the mindset of turning your environment into your identity — not letting it define your limits.

      Shop the collection

      02 — In his words

      I'm Du Antonio, a Toronto-born artist and entrepreneur behind Screw Face Product — a brand built from real experiences, culture, and the environments that shaped me.

      SFP represents more than clothing or music. It's a mindset and a reflection of where you come from, what you've been through, and who you've become. Every piece and every creative project is designed to carry that same authenticity.

      I grew up between Toronto, East Atlanta and Broward County. Three environments, three sets of lessons, and everything that came with them. SFP was created as a reflection of those places and the experiences that came out of them.

      Du Antonio — Founder, SFP
